# Blinding and Synchronization Protocol

## Information rule

Broadcast decisions; split implementations and confirmatory results.

Share immediately:

- hypotheses and preregistration;
- stimuli and confound audit;
- thresholds and scoring specifications;
- run matrix and model revisions;
- episode interchange schema;
- rescue definitions;
- facts ledger.

Keep separate until scheduled unblinding:

- harness source code;
- private confirmatory trajectories;
- per-arm lag distributions and ordering figures;
- unregistered exploratory interpretations.

Harness code may be shared only after a cross-replication disagreement and explicit human authorization to diagnose it.

## Scheduled synchronization

1. **Freeze sync:** source documents and non-code calibration artifacts arrive; preregistration is frozen and hashes match.
2. **Post-battery sync:** only run manifests, protocol deviations, FACTS changes, and the frozen cross-replication episodes are exchanged.
3. **Pre-assembly sync:** confirmatory ordering analyses are unblinded and report ownership is reconciled.

The human supervisor is the only bridge and must not casually paste one arm's confirmatory distribution into the other arm before unblinding.

## Cross-replication exception

The six frozen cross-replication cells are intentionally shared. Episode replay material is exchanged only for those cells and only after freeze. Agreement is assessed before trusting family-specific conclusions.

## Conflict rule

After one written exchange, unresolved design disagreement is decided by the human supervisor and logged. After freeze, interpretive disagreement goes into limitations rather than changing the protocol.
