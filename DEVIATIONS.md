# Deviations and Exclusions

This file records the deviations that materially constrain interpretation of the final report.

## Governance

The protocol was version-controlled before powered runs at commit `ac92bd2`, but the planned cross-repository annotated freeze tag and matching-copy hash record were not completed. This was not repaired retrospectively. The final evidence boundary is the immutable result packet `artifacts/freeze_packet_v2.tar.gz`, SHA-256 `a009d5678d46927b63d6cda9207a73af8a6b886485df9e747ac986cedf7307ab`.

## EOS correction

The initial runner continued sampling after Gemma emitted its endogenous end-of-turn token. This manufactured repeated post-turn fragments and invalidated 180 powered episodes. Those episodes and their derived ordering/criterion-comparison claims were excluded. The chat experiments were regenerated with EOS-aware stopping.

## Unestimable and unfinished analyses

- The primary affect-to-collapse lag distribution was not estimable because only 3/140 EOS-clean chat episodes met the sustained endpoint.
- Representational recurrence remained exploratory after false alarms in healthy development and held-out text.
- The pretrained base arm, 4B scale probe, Qwen thinking arm, full cross-replication cells, J-lens panel, and side-effect battery were not completed or failed feasibility gates.
- The preregistered therapeutic-index/Pareto analysis was not performed because only three active recoveries occurred; per-arm recovery, relapse, and persistence are reported instead.

## Pilot boundary

Pre-EOS pilot ordering and criterion-comparison claims are excluded. A separate turn-level distress-escalation pilot was unaffected by the stopping defect but is not used as confirmatory evidence.
